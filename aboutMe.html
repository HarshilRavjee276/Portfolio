About Me

Why I chose IT

// origin

I've always been drawn to figuring out how things work and then rebuilding them so they work better — that curiosity is what pulled me toward Information Technology. What confirmed it was seeing how much of a difference a well-built piece of software can make to someone's day, whether that's a student checking their results or a business running its systems smoothly. IT gave me a way to combine that problem-solving instinct with something practical and in-demand, and the Higher Certificate in Mobile Application and Web Development let me test that instinct on real projects from day one.

Career goals

// direction

My immediate goal is to build on my Higher Certificate with a full Bachelor of Computer and Information Sciences in Application Development, specialising further in application development and systems architecture. Longer term, I want to work as a mobile or full-stack application developer, contributing to products that are genuinely useful to the people who use them, and eventually take on more responsibility in designing how systems are structured, not just how they're coded.
